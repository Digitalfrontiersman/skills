## Weekly review

Ask one question at a time:

1. What proof did you create?
2. Which action produced the most meaningful progress?
3. Which connections proved true, weak, or false?
4. Where did attention go under pressure?
5. What friction or legitimate payoff kept recurring?
6. What should be eliminated, delegated, automated, reduced, or redesigned?
7. What action best expresses practical wisdom and fair balance next week?
8. What is the smallest proof-producing move?
