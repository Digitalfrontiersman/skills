# Audit Questions

Ask only questions that cannot be answered from connected systems. Do not block read-only inspection while waiting.

## Scope

- Which ad account, campaigns, and dates are in scope?
- Should inactive, archived, duplicate, and draft assets be included?
- Is this a pre-launch check, live diagnosis, or post-mortem?

## Economics

- What is the offer, price, gross margin, capacity, and deadline?
- What is the primary conversion and target acquisition cost?
- What actual purchases, revenue, refunds, booked calls, attended calls, and attendance occurred?
- Which backend is authoritative for each outcome?

## Funnel And Tracking

- Where should each ad send the prospect?
- Which pixel/dataset and event should optimize delivery?
- Are UTMs, CRM source fields, and purchase events expected?
- Were any tracking, domain, event, CRM, or login problems known during the campaign?

## Lead Handling

- Where did leads arrive: Meta Leads Center, CRM, email, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or a spreadsheet?
- Who owned first contact, and what response-time target existed?
- How many calls, texts, emails, and messages were attempted?
- What scripts, automations, reminders, and escalation rules were active?
- What objections or misunderstandings repeatedly appeared?

## Creative And Audience

- Which creatives and claims were intentionally tested?
- Were different formats supplied for Feed, Stories, Reels, and other placements?
- Which geographies, languages, ages, exclusions, custom audiences, and retargeting windows were intended?
- Did any creative attract attention unrelated to the offer?

## Access And Evidence

- Can Codex inspect Ads Manager and download reports?
- Can Codex access or receive exports from CRM, checkout, calendar, messaging, analytics, and attendance systems?
- Are phone and email permitted for reconciliation, and should the final report redact personal data?

## Minimal Blocking Question Set

When context is thin, ask only:

1. What business result and date range should be audited?
2. What were the actual sales/revenue and target acquisition cost?
3. Which backend contains purchases and lead follow-up history?
4. May current campaigns be changed, or is the task read-only?
