# Privacy

Higgsfield Goblin does not operate a separate service, database, analytics system, or data-collection endpoint. It processes information the user chooses to provide inside the AI product hosting the skill.

## Data boundaries

- Do not provide credentials, authentication tokens, payment details, private keys, confidential client material, or unnecessary personal data in prompts or production packets.
- Character, face, and voice assets may contain biometric or personally identifying information. Use them only with appropriate consent and authority.
- Manual planning mode does not require a Higgsfield connection.
- When MCP, CLI, web, search, or another connected tool is used, prompts, reference media, scripts, and generated outputs may be transmitted to that provider. Their retention, training, billing, and privacy terms apply.
- The skill must not infer permission to authenticate, train an identity, upload private media, generate paid assets, publish, share, or retain information outside the current task.
- Preserve only the minimum task-relevant production data. Remove sensitive metadata from public deliverables when appropriate.

Users remain responsible for reviewing the privacy and retention settings of Codex, Hermes, Higgsfield, and any other connected service.

## Public package

The public repository contains no user generations, authentication state, private source media, client briefs, account details, or generation history. The source map points only to public documentation and public educational pages.
