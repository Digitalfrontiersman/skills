# Higgsfield Goblin release brief

## One-line promise

Turn a rough creative idea into a controlled Higgsfield production plan spanning direction, prompts, continuity, generation, editing, and delivery.

## Interpretation

**Moment:** A creator can see the movie or ad in their head, but the work breaks apart across tools, prompts, inconsistent assets, and clips that do not cut together.

**Role:** Higgsfield Goblin is the production creature between the idea and the final cut.

**Mechanism:** It locks the creative north star and reusable assets, designs the edit and shots, routes live tools, compiles prompts, reviews returned footage, and carries approved decisions through finishing.

**Boundary:** It cannot guarantee generation quality, rights clearance, factual claims, model availability, or audience performance, and it cannot spend credits or act on accounts without approval.

## What it does

- Creates treatments, Director DNA, asset bibles, beat maps, shot lists, prompts, and edit plans.
- Routes each shot using live Higgsfield capabilities rather than a frozen model list.
- Preserves character, product, prop, location, camera, light, and editorial continuity.
- Operates through approved MCP/CLI tools or provides a complete manual run packet.
- Reviews footage against observable acceptance criteria and plans finishing and cutdowns.

## What it does not do

- It does not bundle Higgsfield, paid content, credits, models, or private educational material.
- It does not authenticate, upload private media, train identities, generate paid assets, or publish without approval.
- It does not replace human creative judgment, rights review, claims review, or final quality control.

## Trust signals

- Manual public-boundary and affiliation review completed.
- Codex validator, preflight execution, deterministic contract tests, and an independent proof-scenario forward test included.
- No credentials, account data, personal media, private URLs, local machine paths, unpublished plans, or proprietary course corpus included.
- Privacy, security-reporting, exact installation, first-run example, and external-service boundaries documented.
