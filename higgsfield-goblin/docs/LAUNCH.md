## Launch post

You know the moment: the idea looks incredible in your head, but the generated shots drift, the product mutates, and nothing cuts together.

Meet **Higgsfield Goblin**—the production creature between your idea and the final cut.

Give it a film, ad, trailer, product, song, script, or rough visual seed. It locks the creative north star, builds the cast/location/product bible, designs the edit, writes shot-specific Higgsfield prompts, routes the live tools, checks continuity, and plans sound, color, captions, and delivery. If Higgsfield is not connected, it still produces a complete manual run packet.

It does not pretend that AI generation is deterministic magic. It will not install tools, train a likeness, spend credits, make unsupported claims, or publish without approval. Human taste, rights, and final review remain in charge.

Try it on one 15-second piece where product or character consistency actually matters. Then share which production decision saved the most wasted generations.

## Proof-driven demo

**Input:** A 15-second vertical premium black coffee-can ad in a tense rain-soaked city, with exact product fidelity, technique-based cinematic references, and no Higgsfield connection.

**Proof to show:** The response declares manual planning mode; defines a north star and anti-goal; builds an immutable product truth-sheet plan; maps the 15-second edit; writes shot prompts with camera, light, action, audio, and product constraints; provides approval criteria; and recommends testing the highest-risk continuity shot first. No tool, credit, or generation success is invented.

- Run the Codex skill validator against `higgsfield-goblin/`.
- Review official Higgsfield MCP, CLI, Academy, blog, and model schemas before changing routing claims.
- Record material behavior changes in `PUBLIC_RELEASE.md`.
- Route sensitive reports through `SECURITY.md`; use public issues only for non-sensitive bugs and ideas.
