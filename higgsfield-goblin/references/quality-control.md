# Quality control

## Review protocol

Watch once for story and emotion without pausing. Watch again for technical defects. Review frame-by-frame only after identifying a likely issue. Compare against the shot's narrative job and acceptance criteria, not against abstract “AI quality.”

## Scorecard

Score 1–5 and note evidence:

1. story/information clarity
2. emotional and performance truth
3. character identity and anatomy
4. product/prop geometry and brand accuracy
5. location, wardrobe, light, and continuity
6. camera instruction and composition
7. motion, physics, contact, and temporal stability
8. dialogue, lip-sync, ambience, and sound perspective
9. edit utility: handles, endpoint, screen direction, transition
10. platform and safe-area fit

Automatic failure for an ad master: false claim, unreadable/wrong brand mark, changed product geometry, non-consenting likeness/voice, broken anatomy at the focal moment, or a missing CTA/product proof required by the brief.

## Verdicts

- **Approve:** meets the narrative job and quality bar.
- **Approve with post fix:** defect is cheaper and safer to repair in editing, cleanup, sound, grade, or graphics.
- **Revise:** a focused prompt/reference/settings change is likely to solve it.
- **Salvage:** use a partial range, crop, freeze, speed change, insert, or cutaway.
- **Kill:** wrong concept, unusable continuity, or repeated expensive failure; redesign the shot.

## Failure diagnosis

### Identity drift

Check reference quality, number of competing characters, occlusion, motion complexity, shot length, and whether Cast/Soul ID is the correct route. Simplify action before adding adjectives.

### Product mutation

Return to a truth sheet, reduce rotations/occlusion, specify immutable geometry and label orientation, create a product-only hero frame, or composite/place the product with an editing workflow.

### Motion artifacts

Shorten duration, reduce simultaneous actions, define start/action/end, choose one camera move, improve contact and mass cues, or switch to a model with better support for the motion class.

### Camera ignored

Use a standard move name plus direction, start position, endpoint, speed, and relationship to the subject. Remove conflicting shot instructions.

### Plastic or generic image

Improve the location and light source; describe material behavior, practicals, imperfect surfaces, exposure, skin texture, and capture characteristics. Remove stacks of empty quality terms.

### Weak performance

Direct objective, subtext, eyeline, breath, pauses, micro-actions, and reaction timing. Shorten dialogue and isolate coverage.

### Sequence does not cut

Repair screen direction, eyelines, action endpoints, shot-size progression, audio bridges, and visual handles. Generate a connective insert or transition only when the story needs it.

## Final delivery check

- no placeholder or accidental generated text
- correct logo, product, spelling, claims, prices, and CTA
- consent and rights recorded
- dialogue intelligible; music/SFX licensed and balanced
- shots matched before final grade
- captions accurate and safe-area compliant
- aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, codec, duration, and file size match destination
- clean master and captioned/cutdown versions retained
- prompts, assets, generations, approvals, and provenance archived
