- Higgsfield credentials, OAuth state, account configuration, credits, entitlements, and generation history.
- User reference images, identities, voices, product files, footage, outputs, and private briefs.
- Paid or access-controlled courses, downloadable proprietary prompt libraries, and copied video transcripts.
- Automatic installers, background processes, hidden network calls, and automatic publishing.
- Claims of affiliation with or endorsement by Higgsfield, Inc.
