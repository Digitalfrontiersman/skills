# Privacy

IDEA CHAIN does not operate a separate service, database, analytics system, or data-collection endpoint. It processes information the user chooses to provide inside the AI product hosting the skill.

## Data boundaries

- Do not provide credentials, authentication tokens, payment information, private keys, confidential client material, or unnecessary personal data as idea inputs.
- Fast Mode normally works from the supplied context and stable model knowledge.
- Pro and God Modes may use search or connected research tools. Queries and retrieved material are therefore subject to the privacy practices of the hosting AI product, search provider, and any connected service.
- The skill should use only the minimum task-relevant information needed to build the chain.
- The skill must not infer permission to publish, share, store, or send private information.

Users remain responsible for reviewing the privacy and retention settings of the product in which they install or invoke IDEA CHAIN.
