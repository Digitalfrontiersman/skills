# IDEA CHAIN release brief

## One-line promise

Turn one rough seed into a pressure-tested chain of major ideas that reaches one clear goal.

## Interpretation

**Moment:** A builder has the start of something promising but cannot yet see the coherent whole.

**Role:** IDEA CHAIN is a creative fuse.

**Mechanism:** It defines the start and goal, then makes 3-5 major ideas causally ignite one another until the goal is reached.

**Boundary:** It cannot guarantee originality, commercial success, factual completeness, or replace domain-specific professional judgment.

## What it does

- Offers Fast, Pro, and God research depths.
- Produces one connected direction instead of disconnected brainstorm fragments.
- Pressure-tests truth, tension, edge, causality, weak links, and compression.
- Expands or executes a selected chain without losing the original goal.

## What it does not do

- It does not silently change the host model or reasoning setting.
- It does not make consequential publishing, spending, access, deletion, legal, financial, or safety decisions for the user.
- It does not claim that research is complete when sources or tools are unavailable.

## Trust signals

- Manual public-boundary review completed.
- Deterministic contract tests included and passing.
- No credentials, personal data, client material, private URLs, local machine paths, unpublished plans, or proprietary assets included.
- Privacy and security-reporting documents included.
- Exact installation and first-run examples documented.
