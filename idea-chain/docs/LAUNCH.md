## Launch post

You know the moment: the idea has energy, but everything after it is still fog.

Meet **IDEA CHAIN**--a creative fuse for builders who need direction, not another pile of brainstorm fragments.

Give it a business, product, campaign, page, story, event, or rough thought. It defines the start and goal, then builds 3-5 major ideas where every link unlocks the next. Choose Fast for velocity, Pro for focused research, or God for deep research and pressure-testing.

It does not pretend to guarantee originality, market success, or perfect truth. It gives you a coherent, inspectable chain that you can challenge, expand, or turn into real work.

Try it on one idea you cannot stop thinking about. Then share which link changed the direction.

## Proof-driven demo

**Input:** "Create a launch concept for an AI workshop aimed at first-time founders."

**Run:** Pro Mode + brutal reality check.

**Proof to show:** The output contains one `START -> GOAL`, 3-5 numbered major ideas, explicit `This unlocks ->` handoffs, researched claims with citations when needed, and a final `This achieves ->` tied to the goal.

## Maintenance

- Repeat the public-boundary review before every release.
- Run `python -m unittest discover -s idea-chain/tests -v`.
- Run the Codex skill validator against `idea-chain/`.
- Record material behavior changes in `PUBLIC_RELEASE.md`.
- Route sensitive reports through `SECURITY.md`; use public issues only for non-sensitive bugs and ideas.
